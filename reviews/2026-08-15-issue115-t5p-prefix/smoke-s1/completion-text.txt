# Placement Decision

I would add the new rule as a second bullet point under **"Timeouts are explicit,"** immediately after the existing timeout rule. Here's the edited region:

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
- **Total elapsed time is capped independently.** A per-attempt timeout
  with unbounded attempts can still hang the caller; declare both the
  attempt cap and the wall-clock cap at the call site.

## 3. Recording outcomes
```

**Rationale:** The new rule directly extends the "Timeouts are explicit" principle by addressing a critical gap—it prevents the false security of having per-attempt timeouts without a total elapsed-time bound. Placing it as a sibling bullet emphasizes that wall-clock capping is a separate, mandatory concern from per-attempt timeouts, not a detail subordinate to them.
