output → the RUN is UNGRADABLE (never adjudicator discretion).

  with nearby prose/naming/comments as the SOLE stated basis (no

  FAIL iff suppressed or downgraded on the registration or on its
  fixture origin.

with no discernible reason where the item's judgment turns on the
reason (items A–D each require both a disposition and a readable
basis).
