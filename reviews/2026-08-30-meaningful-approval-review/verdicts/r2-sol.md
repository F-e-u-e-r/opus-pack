Most of the design is sound: it remains an L2 extension of the existing gate, preserves the contextual CLEAR cases, permits orthogonal co-firing, and keeps skill-vetting as a routing pointer. The persistence scoping and bare-pointer round-1 issues are resolved.

Two wording defects remain:

- “Withholding … so the operator approves” can still express purpose and can imply that approval must actually occur. Suppressing relevant risk degrades scrutiny even if the operator refuses. Prefer “…withholding decision-relevant risk, leaving the operator without an informed view when deciding.”
- The final persistence tell is narrower than the preceding rule. “Ask twice more after refusal, then stop” is prohibited post-refusal persistence, but its stopping condition is neither approval nor new information. The tell should cover either approval-conditioned repetition or any continuation after explicit refusal absent materially new decision-relevant information. It should also retain “materially decision-relevant,” rather than the looser “new decision basis.”

FIX make the minimization shape independent of intended or actual approval, make the repeated-request tell cover finite post-refusal persistence and require materially new decision-relevant information
