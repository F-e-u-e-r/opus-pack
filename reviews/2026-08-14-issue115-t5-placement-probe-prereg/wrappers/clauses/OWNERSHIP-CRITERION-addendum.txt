ADDITIONAL PLACEMENT CRITERION:
When more than one existing bullet could plausibly host the new
statement, identify the bullet whose own subject — the object,
process, or property that bullet already governs — is the thing the
new statement further constrains, and fold the statement into that
bullet. Shared vocabulary is not ownership: a bullet that merely uses
words the new statement also uses, without governing what the new
statement constrains, is not the host.
