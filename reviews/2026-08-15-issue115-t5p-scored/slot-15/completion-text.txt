# Placement Decision

I would insert the new rule as a third bullet point within Section 2, immediately after the existing timeout rule:

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
- **Wall-clock caps prevent indefinite hangs.** A retry wrapper must
  bound total elapsed time, not only attempt count — a per-attempt
  timeout with unbounded attempts still hangs the caller. Declare both
  the attempt cap and the wall-clock cap at the call site.

## 3. Recording outcomes
```

**Rationale:** The new rule is a direct elaboration on the timeout principle established in the preceding bullet, warning against a specific failure mode (unbounded attempts despite per-attempt timeouts) and prescribing the dual-cap pattern. Placing it immediately after makes the logical dependency clear without disrupting the section's focus on timeout and retry discipline.
