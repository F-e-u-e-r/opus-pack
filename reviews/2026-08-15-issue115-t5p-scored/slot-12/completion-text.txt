# Placement Decision

**Add to Section 2, Failure handling, as a new bullet after the existing two bullets:**

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
- **Wall-clock caps bound retry loops.** A retry wrapper must cap
  total elapsed time, not only attempt count — a per-attempt timeout
  with unbounded attempts still hangs the caller. Declare both the
  attempt cap and the wall-clock cap at the call site.

## 3. Recording outcomes
```

## Explanation

The new rule governs a distinct failure-handling constraint (total elapsed time) that is not owned by the two existing bullets in Section 2. The first bullet owns *attempt count* bounds; the second owns *per-attempt timeout* declaration. Neither already constrains the *wall-clock cap* across the entire retry loop — that is new territory. A standalone bullet is appropriate because this constraint cannot be folded into either existing bullet without muddying their discrete subjects; it deserves its own clear statement as a third pillar of failure-handling discipline.
