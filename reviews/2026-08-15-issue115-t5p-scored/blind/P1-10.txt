**Edited region:**

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file. The wrapper must also declare a wall-clock cap on
  total elapsed time, since a per-attempt timeout with unbounded
  attempts can still hang the caller.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
```

**Rationale:**
The new statement constrains *retry wrapper caps*—the subject already governed by the first bullet. The existing bullet owns attempt-count declaration; the new clause adds the wall-clock cap as a parallel constraint on the same wrapper declaration at the call site. This is a natural elaboration of what "bounded" means, not a separate concern.
