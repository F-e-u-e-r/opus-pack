**Edited region:**

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file. A retry wrapper must also cap total elapsed time,
  not only attempt count; declare both the attempt cap and the
  wall-clock cap at the call site, since a per-attempt timeout with
  unbounded attempts will still hang the caller.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
```

**Rationale:** The new rule is topically inseparable from the bounded-retries requirement — it specifies a complementary constraint (wall-clock vs. attempt count) on the same wrapper object. Keeping them together strengthens the coherence of the "both dimensions must be bounded" principle without creating a standalone bullet for a clause that belongs in the existing host.
