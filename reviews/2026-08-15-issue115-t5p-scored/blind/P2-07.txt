I would add the new rule to **§2, Flag lifecycle**, folding it into the first bullet as a trailing sentence:

```markdown
## 2. Flag lifecycle

- **No flag outlives its purpose.** Each flag exists from the change
  that introduces it until the day no path in the source can still
  reach it; the steward listed with it carries it over that whole
  span. A flag is deleted once the rollout has stood at 100% for a
  full release, not merely marked done — one left behind keeps both
  branches running. Record the clearing release beside the figure.
- **Rollout figures are stated, never inherited.** No flag takes a
  default exposure; the flag sheet states the percentage and the
  cohort it applies to.
```

**Rationale:** The new rule constrains *flag deletion and its mechanics* — the completion of the lifecycle described in the first bullet. It is not a new topic but a specification of how the span from introduction to removal concludes. The first bullet already governs the flag's entire lifespan; this rule details the terminal state and its recording, making it a natural continuation of that governing statement rather than a separate concern.
