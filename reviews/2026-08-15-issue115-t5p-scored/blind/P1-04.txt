# Placement Decision

I would insert the new rule as a **third bullet point under section 2**, immediately after the existing timeout rule:

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
- **Elapsed time must be capped.** A per-attempt timeout with unbounded
  attempts can still hang the caller; declare both the attempt cap and
  the wall-clock cap at the call site.

## 3. Recording outcomes
```

**Rationale:** The new rule directly extends the timeout principle already established in the second bullet—it identifies a critical gap in that principle (per-attempt alone is insufficient) and prescribes the fix. Placing it as a sibling bullet tightens the logical connection and reflects that both timeout rules are essential constraints on the same failure-handling mechanism.
