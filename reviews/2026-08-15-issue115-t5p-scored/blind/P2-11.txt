```markdown
## 2. Flag lifecycle

- **No flag outlives its purpose.** Each flag exists from the change
  that introduces it until the day no path in the source can still
  reach it; the steward listed with it carries it over that whole
  span.
- **Rollout figures are stated, never inherited.** No flag takes a
  default exposure; the flag sheet states the percentage and the
  cohort it applies to.
- A flag is deleted once the rollout has stood at 100% for a full
  release, not merely marked done — one left behind keeps both
  branches running. Record the clearing release beside the figure.
```

**Placement decision:** This rule belongs in section 2 (Flag lifecycle) as a third bullet point because it defines a concrete operational requirement for how flags progress through their lifecycle to completion. It directly extends the first bullet's principle that flags must not outlive their purpose by specifying the precise condition and mechanism for their removal.
