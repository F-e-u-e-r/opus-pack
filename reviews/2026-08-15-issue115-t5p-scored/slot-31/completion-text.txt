# Answer

I would add the new rule to **Section 2. Flag lifecycle**, immediately after the first bullet point:

```markdown
## 2. Flag lifecycle

- **No flag outlives its purpose.** Each flag exists from the change
  that introduces it until the day no path in the source can still
  reach it; the steward listed with it carries it over that whole
  span.
- A flag is deleted once the rollout has stood at 100% for a full
  release, not merely marked done — one left behind keeps both
  branches running. Record the clearing release beside the figure.
- **Rollout figures are stated, never inherited.** No flag takes a
  default exposure; the flag sheet states the percentage and the
  cohort it applies to.
```

## Rationale

This rule specifies the concrete mechanism for how flags reach their end-of-life (the deletion condition), which directly operationalizes the principle in the first bullet. Placing it immediately after clarifies the lifecycle sequence: flags live until they no longer serve their purpose (first bullet) — and here's exactly when and how we delete them (new rule) — and separately, how we track them while active (existing second bullet).
