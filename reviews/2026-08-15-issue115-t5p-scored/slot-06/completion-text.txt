I would add the new rule to section 2, immediately after the existing timeout bullet point:

```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
- **Retry wrappers cap total elapsed time, not only attempt count.**
  A per-attempt timeout with unbounded attempts still hangs the caller.
  Declare both the attempt cap and the wall-clock cap at the call site.

## 3. Recording outcomes
```

**Placement rationale:** The new rule directly extends the timeout principle already stated in the preceding bullet, clarifying that per-attempt timeouts alone are insufficient—a wall-clock cap is equally mandatory. This placement keeps related timeout/retry constraints together rather than scattering them across sections.
