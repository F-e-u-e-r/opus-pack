**Edited region (§2):**
```markdown
## 2. Failure handling

- **Retries are bounded and jittered.** Every retry wrapper declares
  its maximum attempt count at the call site; backoff is exponential
  with jitter, and a non-retriable error class aborts immediately —
  enumerate those classes beside the wrapper, not in a distant
  constants file.
- **Timeouts are explicit.** No call inherits a library default
  timeout silently; the call site states its per-attempt timeout.
  A retry wrapper must also cap total elapsed time, not only attempt
  count — declare both the per-attempt and wall-clock caps at the
  call site, since a per-attempt timeout with unbounded attempts still
  hangs the caller.
```

**Rationale:** The new rule is a direct refinement of the timeout bullet's own scope (the requirement to state timeout bounds at the call site). It strengthens that bullet by clarifying that *two* bounds are required, not one, and explains the failure mode that makes wall-clock capping non-optional. It belongs as an elaboration on the existing timeout principle, not as a standalone bullet.
